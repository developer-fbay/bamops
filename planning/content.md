## 2. Case files

Intro line: "We can't show you everything. NDAs, mostly. Also pride."

Each card: codename, sector tag, redacted preview, Problem / Fix / Result,
cheeky footnote. **No URLs, hostnames, client names or screenshots** (pending
sign-off — see open questions in `brief.md`).

### case_01 — "The Brain"
- Sector: finance · deal management
- Problem: Deals lived in inboxes, spreadsheets and one person's memory.
  When that person went on holiday, so did the pipeline.
- Fix: A single operating system for every deal — intake to payout — that
  remembers everything and nags people politely.
- Result: One source of truth. Holidays are allowed again.
- Footnote: "Named accurately."

### case_02 — "Comparison engine"
- Sector: fintech · lead gen
- Problem: Matching a business to the right funder meant hours of phone calls
  and gut feel.
- Fix: An engine that cross-checks 30+ sources and returns a shortlist in
  seconds.
- Result: 95% right, 100% certain.
- Footnote: "The other 5% are character-building."

### case_03 — "Partner portal"
- Sector: B2B · introducers
- Problem: Partners sent referrals by email, WhatsApp, and occasionally
  carrier pigeon. Nobody knew who was owed what.
- Fix: A portal where partners submit, track and get paid — no chasing.
- Result: Fewer "just following up" emails. Possibly zero.
- Footnote: "CSV is an API. Everyone insisted."

### case_04 — "A/B Testing" (PLACEHOLDER — real details to come from Jesse)
- Sector: growth · experimentation
- Problem: Decisions were made by whoever spoke loudest in the meeting.
- Fix: A testing setup that splits traffic, tracks what matters and calls a
  winner.
- Result: Opinions are now optional.
- Footnote: "Variant B. It's always variant B."

### case_05 — "Room booking"
- Sector: property · coworking
- Problem: Meeting rooms double-booked, invoices forgotten, doors locked on
  the wrong people.
- Fix: Booking, confirmations, door access and invoicing in one flow.
- Result: Nobody fights over the 3pm slot any more. It's just gone.
- Footnote: "It was gone before you read this."
